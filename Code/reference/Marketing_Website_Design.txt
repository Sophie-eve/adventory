Build a modern, responsive marketing website for a B2B marketing-measurement SaaS 
called "[YOUR BRAND NAME]". It sells media mix modeling and marketing measurement 
to brands and agencies. Style: clean, premium, lots of white space, a calm blue 
or green accent color, rounded cards, subtle animations. Use React + Tailwind CSS 
(or plain HTML/CSS/JS if React isn't available). Front end only; use mock 
handlers for anything that needs a backend.

LAYOUT
- Desktop: a fixed LEFT SIDEBAR (about 260px) plus a main area. The top of the 
  main area holds the center navigation bar.
- Mobile: the sidebar collapses into a hamburger drawer.

LEFT SIDEBAR (top to bottom)
1. Logo.
2. Search bar, with a "Ctrl + K" hint badge (show "⌘ K" on Mac). Pressing 
   Ctrl+K / Cmd+K anywhere focuses it and opens a command-palette modal that 
   searches all pages and menu items below, with keyboard up/down/enter/escape.
3. "Log in" button opening a modal with "Continue with Google" and 
   "Continue with Apple" buttons (UI only, mock the sign-in), plus a note 
   about terms and privacy.
4. "Book a demo" button (prominent, accent color). It opens a modal form with:
   - First name (required)
   - Business website (required, validate as a URL)
   - Phone number (required, validate format)
   - "Are you looking to sign up as a Brand or an Agency?" (required, radio 
     buttons or a toggle: Brand / Agency)
   - Submit button. Show inline validation errors and a success message 
     ("Thanks! We'll be in touch shortly."). Log the data to the console.

CENTER NAVIGATION (top bar of the main area, three items with large dropdown mega-menus 
that open on hover and on click, and are keyboard accessible)

1. PRODUCT
   Left column, "Product":
   - Overview: See the full platform at a glance
   - Media Mix Model: Measure the true impact of every channel
   - Performance: Track and analyze campaign results
   - Optimization: Allocate spend for maximum ROAS
   - Retail: Measure omnichannel retail performance
   - Integrations: Connect your marketing stack seamlessly
   Right column, "Value":
   - Measure halo effects: See how each channel lifts the others
   - Validate measurement sources: Cross-check platform data against real outcomes
   - Scale with confidence: Increase spend knowing what will drive growth

2. SOLUTIONS
   Column "By role":
   - Marketing: Prove marketing's impact on revenue
   - Finance: Tie ad spend to business outcomes
   - Operations: Streamline data and reporting workflows
   Column "By type":
   - Scaling brand: Drive profitable growth online
   - Omnichannel: Measure across every channel
   - Holding Company: Portfolio-wide measurement at scale
   - Agency: Measurement for your clients

3. CUSTOMERS
   - Customer Stories: Deep dives into client results
   - Reviews & Testimonials: Real feedback from brands driving growth

Each menu item has a small icon, a bold title and a one-line description. 
Each item links to its own page (create simple placeholder pages with a title, 
short intro and a "Book a demo" call-to-action).

HOME PAGE (below the nav)
- Hero: headline about proving the true impact of every marketing dollar, 
  a short subheading, a "Book a demo" button, and a clean dashboard-style 
  illustration built from CSS/SVG charts.
- A row of placeholder customer logos.
- Three feature cards (Measure halo effects, Validate measurement sources, 
  Scale with confidence).
- A testimonials section and a final call-to-action banner.
- Footer with links.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop), keyboard accessible, ARIA labels 
  on modals and menus, focus trapping inside modals, Escape closes any modal 
  or menu.
- Smooth hover and open/close animations; no layout shift.
- Clean, commented, componentized code (Sidebar, MegaMenu, SearchPalette, 
  LoginModal, DemoModal, Hero, etc.).
- Use original text, images and branding. Do NOT copy any other company's logo, 
  copyright text or images.
